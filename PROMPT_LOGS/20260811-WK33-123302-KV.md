### 12:33:17 - Response

You're right — I overstated the deploy-all time savings. AMI pipelines run in parallel (see `build-amis-async` — three `build-ami-*` invocations back-to-back but each triggers an async Image Builder execution, then `wait-amis` polls all three). Wall time = max(nginx, php74, php83), not sum. Skipping php74 only helps wall time if php74 was the longest of the three, which it likely wasn't — nginx and php83 have similar package footprints.

**What you actually save:**
- Image Builder pipeline execution charges (pennies per run)
- The mental noise of watching a pipeline you don't care about
- Slightly less CFN drift (no leftover PHP74 AMI in SSM referenced by nothing)

**What you DO save wall time on:**
- The RDS boost (pgloader ceiling drop, potentially 30+ min)
- The parallel fan-out we just added (5-6 min)

That's where the real ~30-40 min end-to-end gains come from. PHP74 skip is a nice-to-have, not a time win.
