## Diagnosis recap — cross-account S3 needs three grants, we had one

| # | Grant | Where | Was it in place before this session? |
|---|-------|-------|---|
| 1 | Jumpbox role can **read** `zi-documents` | Prod IAM (identity policy) | ❌ — added in first attempt this turn |
| 2 | Sandbox `MediaBucket` allows the jumpbox role | Sandbox S3 (bucket policy) | ✅ — added in `98bec6c` last session |
| 3 | Jumpbox role can **write** `sandbox-drupal-media-kv-worxco` | Prod IAM (identity policy) | ❌ — **added just now** |

Cross-account S3 requires the grant on *both* sides. #2 alone (bucket policy) isn't enough — the caller's IAM role also has to allow the action on the target ARN. Missing #3 is why ListBucket failed at Phase 1 even though the sandbox bucket policy explicitly permits it.
