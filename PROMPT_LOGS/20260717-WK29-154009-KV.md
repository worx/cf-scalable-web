**Interpreting the output:**

Fix path: `ALTER USER drupal_user WITH PASSWORD '$DRUPAL_DB_PASS';` to re-sync (this is exactly the "install-drupal.sh makes safe to re-run" rationale at scripts/deploy-host/install-drupal.sh:198-202).
