## Follow-up: `restore-private.sh` should preserve sandbox-specific files

This is a real gap in the design. `/var/www/drupal-private/salt.txt` is **sandbox-managed** (per `docs/memory/salt-persistence-design.md`), not prod-migrated. When restore-private atomic-swaps the whole dir, it clobbers sandbox-managed files with prod's data (or absence thereof).

Two ways to fix — will queue as a task:

**A.** After swap, copy sandbox-managed files from `.BAK` back into place (list of files: salt.txt, maybe others). Simple, invasive-in-script.

**B.** Change restore-private to only unpack into `drupal-private/private-files/` (or similar subdir), leaving `salt.txt` and other sandbox metadata at the top level untouched. Requires prod-side change to `dump-private.sh` to match. Cleaner but bigger.

Take your fix now, we'll discuss which follow-up makes sense once the site is confirmed serving.
