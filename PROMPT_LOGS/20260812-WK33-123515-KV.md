### 12:35:32 - Response

Correct on both counts. Two independent background pipelines running alongside the main critical path:

**1. sync-s3-media (pre-warmed, backgrounded from chain start)**
- Launched: before Phase 1
- Waits for: sandbox media BUCKET to exist (storage-s3 stack CREATE_COMPLETE, ~30s into deploy-all)
- Runs during: entire chain from that point forward
- Joined at: right before Phase 11 (smoke tests need media)
- Why it works: sync-s3-media is S3-to-S3 server-side; nothing in Phases 1-10 depends on the media bucket being populated

**2. destroy-migrate-host (backgrounded from Phase 4)**
- Launched: Phase 4 (right after migrate-db-all-boosted completes)
- Runs during: Phases 5-11
- Joined at: after Phase 11, before "PASSED" banner
- Why it works: migrate-host's job (running MariaDB + pgloader) is done by end of Phase 3; nothing downstream touches it

**One nuance on join timing:** we `wait` for both jobs before printing "PASSED" because we want to catch their exit codes — silently orphaning a failed background job would let bugs hide. Media sync failure aborts the chain (bad — Phase 11 would fail anyway). Destroy failure only WARNs (recoverable — you can `make destroy-migrate-host CONFIRMED=yes` manually to clean up).

**Cleanup safety net:** a `trap` on the tee'd subshell catches EXIT and kills any still-running background jobs — so if the chain fails at, say, Phase 3, the media sync doesn't orphan and keep running for 20 min after we've already bailed.

That's the whole shape. Two parallel tracks that rejoin at natural sync points. Expected savings on the critical path: 3 min from #1, plus 5-20 min from #3 depending on media size — modestly compounded because they overlap different windows.
